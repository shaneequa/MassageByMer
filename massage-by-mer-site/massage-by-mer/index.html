About

Meet Meredith Kubic

Meredith is a graduate of The Institute for Therapeutic Massage. She believes in the deep therapeutic effect relaxation and rest have on the body, and focuses on nervous system regulation during each session.

Her therapeutic massages offer a slow and intentional approach, giving each person an opportunity to slow down, rest, and repair. This emphasis on stress relief and pain reduction allows each client to let go, leaving space for the body's inner wisdom and natural ability to heal.

With an emphasis on compassion, Meredith attunes to each person's specific needs in every appointment. She takes time to speak with every client before and after the massage to provide customized care.
